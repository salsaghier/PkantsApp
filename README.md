Your plants, cared for in one place.

## Our Goal

Planto helps you remember to water your plants and keep track of their care.

## Features

- Add your plants and their care details.
- Choose how often each plant needs watering.
- Receive watering reminders.
- Mark plants as watered and watch your progress.
- Edit or delete plants easily.
- Keep your plants saved when you close the app.

## How to Use
<img width="1536" height="800" alt="Planto app flow showcase" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e040ac53-e4e8-48ff-9e35-20cea735fa7c" />

1. Tap “Set Plant Reminder” to view your plants.
2. Tap + to add a plant.
3. Enter its name and choose its care details.
4. Save your plant.
5. Tap the circle beside it after watering.
Allow notifications to receive reminders.
